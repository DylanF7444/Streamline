    Tracking and toolbar details

    Pin this extension to see time saved today on the focused website. The green dot confirms a recent Hub save. WAIT means waiting, idle or an ineligible page; OFF means paused; ! means a connection problem. Older helpers show REC until updated.

    Today's totals combine sites across Chrome, Edge and Brave in Hub's timezone. The bar shows the top eight sites and groups the rest as Other. Totals update after Hub saves, approximately every five seconds.

    With your permission, only the focused tab's hostname is sent to Personal Hub on this PC. The browser permission also allows tab titles; this extension does not read them. Private tabs are excluded. No page content or full addresses are saved. Hub controls idle detection, exclusions, history and deletion. Privacy masking hides time details.
