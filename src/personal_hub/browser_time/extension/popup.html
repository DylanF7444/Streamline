Browser screen time

With your permission, this extension reads the focused tab's address to send only its hostname to Personal Hub on this PC. Browser permission also allows access to tab titles; this extension does not read them.

Requires Hub's recording opt-in. Private tabs are excluded. No browsing history, page content or full addresses are saved. Hub controls idle detection, exclusions, history and deletion.

Pin this extension to the browser toolbar to see its badge: REC means Hub confirmed saved time; WAIT means waiting or an ineligible page; OFF means paused; ! means a connection problem. Hover for details.
